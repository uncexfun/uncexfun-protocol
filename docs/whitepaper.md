# Uncex Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

**Vision.** Access to useful software assistants should not depend on a card number and a renewal reminder. A position in a token should be enough to come back, again and again, and be let in.

**Mission.** Build Uncex on Solana so that holding the token replaces the recurring invoice: simple to understand, open to inspect, and honest about what is and is not confirmed. Anything not yet stated is shown as pending rather than guessed.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation

Publish the site, the market board and the register. Confirm the contract address and social accounts and switch their slots live.

### Phase 2: Access

State the minimum position, the session cycle details and the assistant roster. Turn the size slider outputs from pending to real estimates.

### Phase 3: Reach

Open a public channel for questions, publish the repository, and document how sessions are issued so anyone can check them.

### Phase 4: Depth

Widen the roster, tune the cycle based on observed use, and publish regular transparency notes.
